A good pipeline failure should tell the engineer what changed, what failed, and what the safest next step is.

## Things To Cover Later

- Separate validation, build, test, scan, and release stages.
- Keep logs focused and searchable.
- Make rollback paths obvious.
- Use approvals where risk justifies them.
