Good runbooks are practical. They do not try to explain the entire system from first principles. They help someone make the next correct move when the alert is already noisy.

## Draft Shape

- Start with symptoms and impact.
- Include commands that are safe to run.
- Add rollback or escalation paths.
- Keep the owner and review date visible.
