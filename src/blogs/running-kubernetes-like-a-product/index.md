---

Kubernetes platforms are more successful when they are treated as products rather than shared machinery. The users are developers, operators, and incident responders.

That means platform work should include documentation, sensible defaults, upgrade plans, ownership boundaries, and feedback loops. Reliability improves when people know what the platform promises and how to use it well.

The best internal platforms reduce cognitive load while still giving teams enough control to ship responsibly.
